## 💡 Key Learnings

1. **Audience first:** one product can need three different messages — a student, a professional and a parent buy for different reasons.
2. **Every platform has a job:** Instagram for reach, LinkedIn for credibility, YouTube for depth, the website for leads.
3. **Value before selling:** a free roadmap, a challenge and a workshop do the persuading; the offer comes last.
4. **Measure the whole funnel:** reach and likes are not enough — CPL, conversion rate and CAC show what actually works.
