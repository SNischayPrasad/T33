# 6. Content Pillars

Four pillars keep content useful and stop the feed from turning into ads. Planned share vs what the 30-day calendar actually delivers (60 posts):

| Pillar | Planned | In calendar | Purpose | Example topics | Main persona |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Educational - 'Learn' | 35% | 37% (22) | Teach skills, build trust and saves | Roadmaps, speed builds, Git basics, LLMs explained, portfolio tips | Rahul, Priya |
| Informational - 'Know' | 20% | 18% (11) | Industry and career context | Hiring trends, internship timelines, service vs product, 2027 skills outlook | Priya, Srinivas |
| Engagement - 'Connect' | 25% | 27% (16) | Community, comments, shares, UGC | Memes, polls, quizzes, #BuildIn30, live Q&A, student spotlights | Rahul |
| Promotional - 'Grow' | 20% max | 18% (11) | Drive sign-ups and sales | Workshop invites, cohort launch, early-bird offer, learner stories | All three |

- Rule of thumb: no more than one promotional post in every five on Instagram.
- Every promotional post must still give value (a demo, a story, a clear fee breakdown).
