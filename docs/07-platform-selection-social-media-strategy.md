# 7. Platform Selection & Social Media Strategy

## Platform selection

| Platform | Selected? | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Yes - primary | Where students spend most of their time; Reels give the cheapest reach |
| LinkedIn | Yes | Professionals (Priya) and student job-seekers; builds credibility |
| YouTube | Yes | Long-form tutorials build deep trust; Telugu content reaches parents |
| Website / Blog | Yes | SEO traffic, landing pages and lead capture we own |
| WhatsApp Channel + Email | Yes - support | Reminders, nurturing leads, reaching parents |
| Facebook | Light | Repurpose posts and Telugu videos for parents only |
| X (Twitter) | No | Low audience fit for the budget |

## Platform-by-platform plan

| Platform | Audience | Content focus | Formats | Frequency | Best time (IST) | Main KPI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Students 18-23 | Relatable, quick learning, challenges | Reels, carousels, stories, Lives | Daily post + daily stories | 7-9 PM | Reach, engagement rate, saves |
| LinkedIn | Professionals, final-years | Career advice, credibility, events | Text posts, PDF carousels, polls, events, newsletter | 4 posts/week | 8-10 AM Mon-Fri | Engagement rate, event sign-ups |
| YouTube | Students, parents (Telugu) | In-depth tutorials, replays | Long-form + Shorts (re-cut Reels) | 1 long video + 3 Shorts/week | Sun 11 AM | Watch time, subscribers |
| Website / Blog | Searchers with intent | SEO guides, landing pages | Blogs, landing pages, lead forms | 2 posts/week | Publish 11 AM | Organic sessions, conversion rate |
| WhatsApp / Email | Leads, parents | Reminders, nurture, offers | Broadcasts, newsletters | 2-3/week | Evening | Open rate, click rate |

Best times are starting hypotheses; after two weeks they are reviewed against Instagram Insights and LinkedIn Analytics.

## How the platforms work together

- **Repurposing loop:** 1 YouTube tutorial -> 3 Reels/Shorts -> 1 Instagram carousel -> 1 LinkedIn PDF carousel -> 1 blog post.
- **Different copy per platform:** casual, emoji-friendly on Instagram; professional and story-led on LinkedIn; keyword-led on the website.
- **Community management:** reply to comments within 2 hours (9 AM-10 PM); keyword DM automation ('CODE', 'NEWYEAR').
- **Paid support:** boost the top 20% of organic posts each week; retarget video viewers and site visitors with workshop and offer ads.
