# Executive Summary

**LearnLoop** is a fictional Hyderabad-based EdTech start-up that runs live, mentor-led, project-based courses in Full-Stack Development, Data Science and AI for college students and early-career professionals. This document is its complete digital marketing strategy for a 30-day campaign running **1-30 December 2026**, built around the brand promise *Learn. Build. Get Hired. Repeat.*

The campaign moves the audience through four weekly themes - awareness, community, lead generation and conversion - across Instagram (primary), LinkedIn, YouTube and the website/blog. It is planned with a budget of Rs 1,50,000 and targets 2,00,000 unique accounts reached, 2,000 leads and 150 paid enrolments for the January 2027 cohorts.

- **Audience:** 3 personas - a 2nd-year B.Tech student, a service-company developer switching to AI, and a parent who funds the course.
- **Content mix:** Educational 37%, Informational 18%, Engagement 27%, Promotional 18% - fewer than 1 in 5 posts sells.
- **Calendar:** 60 planned posts in 30 days - 30 Instagram, 17 LinkedIn, 9 website pages and 4 YouTube videos, each written separately for its platform.
- **Hero campaigns:** #BuildIn30 project challenge, free 'Build Your First AI App' workshop and the 'New Year, New Skill' early-bird offer.
- **Measurement:** 20+ KPIs across the funnel, tracked weekly in Google Sheets with UTM-tagged links.
