# 9. Campaign Ideas

| Campaign | Objective | Platforms | How it works | Timing | Success measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stop Watching, Start Building | Awareness | Instagram, YouTube | Relatable reels about tutorial hell + free Full-Stack Roadmap 2027 PDF as lead magnet | Week 1 | Reach 60k; 600 PDF downloads |
| #BuildIn30 Challenge | Engagement, UGC | Instagram, LinkedIn | Post one mini-project a week with #BuildIn30; weekly spotlights; 10 free course seats for winners | Weeks 2-3 | 300 submissions |
| Build Your First AI App (free workshop) | Lead generation | All + landing page | 90-min live build on Sat 19 Dec; registration form; reminders by WhatsApp/email; replay on YouTube | Week 3 | 1,500 sign-ups; 40% attend |
| New Year, New Skill | Conversion | Instagram, LinkedIn, email, ads | 25% early-bird off January cohorts + EMI from Rs 999/month; countdown stories; closes 31 Dec | Week 4 | 150 enrolments |
| Campus Ambassadors | Reach + referrals | WhatsApp, Instagram | 20 students across 20 colleges share referral codes; earn certificate + stipend per enrolment | Whole month | 20 referral enrolments |
| Telugu Tech Talks | Trust with parents | YouTube, Facebook, WhatsApp | Short Telugu explainers on careers, fees and outcomes; counsellor call CTA | Weekly | 100 counselling calls |
| Micro-influencer collabs | Reach + credibility | Instagram, YouTube | 5 Telugu/English tech creators (10k-100k followers) review a project-building session | Weeks 2-4 | Rs 30 or less per lead |
| Code Meme Mondays | Engagement habit | Instagram | A recurring weekly meme slot the audience expects and shares | Every Monday | Shares per post |
