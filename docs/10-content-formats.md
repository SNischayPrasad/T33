# 10. Content Formats

| Format | Platform | Best for | Best practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reels / Shorts | Instagram, YouTube | Reach, awareness | 15-45 sec, hook in the first 2 sec, captions on, trending audio |
| Carousels | Instagram | Saves, education | 7-10 slides, bold cover headline, CTA on last slide |
| Stories (polls, quizzes, countdowns) | Instagram | Daily engagement, reminders | Daily; use stickers; save highlights for workshop and FAQs |
| Instagram Live / workshops | Instagram, YouTube | Trust, leads | Announce 3 days ahead; reminders; pin replay |
| Text posts | LinkedIn | Thought leadership | Short lines, story-led, question at the end, 3-5 hashtags |
| Document (PDF) carousels | LinkedIn | Saves, reach | 8-12 pages, one idea per page |
| Polls | LinkedIn, Instagram | Research + engagement | Share results in a follow-up post |
| LinkedIn Events & newsletter | LinkedIn | Registrations, retention | Clear date/time/value; monthly newsletter |
| Long-form video | YouTube | Deep learning, search | Chapters, keyword-led title, custom thumbnail |
| SEO blog posts | Website | Organic traffic | 1,200-1,800 words, one primary keyword, FAQs, internal links |
| Landing pages | Website | Conversions | One goal, short form, social proof, mobile-first |
| Infographics & memes | Instagram, LinkedIn | Shares | On-brand colours, simple, relatable |
| Testimonials / UGC | All | Social proof | Real names (with consent), specific results |
| Email & WhatsApp broadcasts | Owned channels | Nurture, reminders | Personal tone, one CTA, 2-3 per week |
