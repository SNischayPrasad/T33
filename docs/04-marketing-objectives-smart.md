# 4. Marketing Objectives (SMART)

**Business goal:** fill the January 2027 cohorts with 150 new paying learners while building a brand that students trust.

| # | Objective | Specific, measurable target (by 30 Dec 2026) | Funnel stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build awareness | Reach 2,00,000 unique accounts across Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube (baseline ~15,000/month) | Awareness |
| 2 | Grow the community | Instagram 1,200 -> 5,000 followers; LinkedIn 800 -> 2,500; YouTube 300 -> 1,500 subscribers | Awareness / Interest |
| 3 | Drive engagement | Average engagement rate >= 5% on Instagram and >= 3% on LinkedIn; 300 #BuildIn30 submissions | Interest |
| 4 | Generate leads | 2,000 unique leads (workshop sign-ups + roadmap downloads); landing-page conversion >= 12% | Consideration |
| 5 | Convert to sales | 150 paid enrolments; customer acquisition cost (CAC) <= Rs 1,000 | Conversion |
| 6 | Build SEO foundation | Publish 8 blogs + 1 landing page; 5 target keywords in Google top 30 within 90 days | Long-term awareness |
