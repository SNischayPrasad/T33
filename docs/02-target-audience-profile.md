# 2. Target Audience Profile

LearnLoop has one paying user group with two segments, plus an influencer group who often approves or pays. Each needs a different message and platform.

| Attribute | Primary: Students | Secondary: Early-career professionals | Influencers: Parents |
|---|---|---|---|
| Age | 18-23 | 22-28 | 42-55 |
| Location | Tier-1/2 cities in Telangana, AP, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu | Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Pune | Same regions as students; many in Tier-2/3 towns |
| Occupation | B.Tech / BCA / B.Sc students (2nd-4th year) | Developers, testers, support engineers at IT-services firms (0-4 yrs) | Salaried / small-business owners |
| Budget | Rs 3,000-8,000; needs EMI or parental support | Rs 8,000-25,000; self-funded | Pays for child; values EMI and proof |
| Goals | Internship, first job, portfolio, confidence | Switch to product/AI role, higher salary | Child gets a stable, well-paid job |
| Pain points | Theory-heavy syllabus, tutorial overload, no feedback, placement anxiety | No time, outdated skills, unsure which skill pays | Fear of scams, too many options, no proof |
| Online behaviour | Instagram Reels, YouTube, WhatsApp groups; evening use (7-11 PM) | LinkedIn on weekday mornings, YouTube long-form at weekends | WhatsApp, Facebook, YouTube (Telugu) |
| Buying triggers | Free workshop, peer reviews, clear roadmap, deadlines | Credible mentors, flexible weekend batch, success stories | Transparent fees, placement stats, call with counsellor |
| Main objection | 'YouTube is free - why pay?' | 'Will I finish it with a full-time job?' | 'Is this legit?' |
