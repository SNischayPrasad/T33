# 11. Hashtag & Keyword Strategy

## Hashtag tiers

| Tier | Purpose | Hashtags |
|---|---|---|
| Branded | Build brand recall, collect UGC | #LearnLoop #LearnBuildGetHired #BuildIn30 + weekly campaign tag |
| Niche | Reach people interested in the topic | #fullstackdeveloper #webdevelopment #pythonprogramming #aiforbeginners #machinelearning #100daysofcode #codinglife #javascript |
| Community / regional | Reach students and local tech circles | #btechlife #engineeringstudents #placementprep #hyderabadtech #telugutech |
| Broad | Extra discovery (use sparingly) | #coding #programming #technology #ai #careers |

## Usage rules by platform

- **Instagram:** 8-12 hashtags per post - 2 branded, 3-4 niche, 2 regional, 2 broad. Rotate niche tags to avoid repetition.
- **LinkedIn:** 3-5 hashtags - brand + campaign + 1-2 topic tags.
- **YouTube:** 3 hashtags in the description; keywords in title, description and tags.
- **Website:** no hashtags - one primary keyword per page plus 2-3 related keywords.

## How keywords are researched and validated

- **Google Trends:** compare terms such as 'AI course' vs 'data science course' vs 'full stack course' for India and Telangana over 12 months; pick rising terms.
- **Keyword tools:** check volume and difficulty in Google Keyword Planner or Ubersuggest; prefer long-tail terms with lower competition.
- **Google autocomplete, 'People also ask' and AnswerThePublic:** collect real questions for blog FAQs.
- **Review monthly** in Google Search Console and update the keyword map.
